ABOUT US

Serving With Care Since 1992

Welcome to Food Care Hotel, a place created around the simple idea that good food and warm hospitality can create wonderful memories.

The business is owned by Nikita Meena and was established in 1992.

We aim to provide our guests with tasty food, friendly service and a comfortable environment for dining and spending time with family and friends.

Our goal is to continue building trust with our customers through quality, hospitality and care.
